Case study: Sal's Home Improvement (practice client)
THE PROBLEM

Estimates ate the whole evening

Every job meant retyping the same structure by hand — scope, materials, labor, pricing — often after hours, with no consistency between quotes and no time left to follow up with the next lead.

THE BUILD

Airtable + Zapier + GPT-4o

Job details land in Airtable. Zapier triggers a workflow that hands the details to GPT-4o, which drafts a formatted, client-ready estimate — no template-wrangling, no blank page.

THE RESULT

Minutes, not evenings

Estimates go out same-day instead of days later, with consistent formatting and pricing logic every time — freeing up hours for the actual work.
